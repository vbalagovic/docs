## The rule

**Credits are used only when Neural Draft's own AI pipeline does work.**
Everything else is covered by the plan and its [limits](/guides/hosted-sites#limits-per-plan):
hosting, pages, deploys, content and image writes through the API or MCP,
forms, bookings, the newsletter, the shop, scheduling and publishing, and
anything your own AI assistant writes.

1 credit = **$0.01**.

## What costs credits

| Work | Credits | About |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Blog post (researched, written, with images) | 60 | $0.60 |
| Translation by Neural Draft, per language (blog post, content key, site) | 10 | $0.10 |
| Crafted static ad (1:1, 4:5 and 9:16) | 50 | $0.50 |
| Reel (vertical, 8–15 s) | 500 | $5.00 |
| Reel voiceover add-on | 10 | $0.10 |
| App Capture (screens and recordings of a web app, for ads) | 200 | $2.00 |

We don't offer AI image or video creation. We craft finished static ads and
reels for your brand, from the brand kit, your own photos and screenshots.
The old `POST /v1/images` (with a prompt) and `POST /v1/videos` endpoints
answer `410 media_generation_moved`.

**Free (0 credits):** `PUT /v1/content/{key}`, `POST`/`PATCH /v1/pages`,
`POST`/`PUT /v1/images` (upload or URL), components, galleries, a blog post you
create with `type: "manual"`, deploys, bookings, orders, forms, webhooks,
every `GET`, and translations your own assistant writes (for example
`translate_site` in its default mode).

## Agencies

Agencies get a quote: from **$190 a month + $49 per client site**, with a
pooled credit allowance shared by all clients and a monthly cap per client.
See [Agencies](/guides/agencies) and [talk to us](https://neuraldraft.io/support?topic=agency#contact).

## At zero

Credits stop at zero: there are no overage charges. A credit-using call
returns `402`:

```json
{
  "type": "https://neuraldraft.com/errors/payment-required",
  "title": "Payment Required",
  "status": 402,
  "detail": "Insufficient credits.",
  "code": "insufficient_credits",
  "cost": 60,
  "balance": 12
}
```

Sites, bookings, forms and the shop keep working. Failed jobs are refunded
automatically.

## Checking usage

<CodeGroup>

```bash cURL
curl https://api.neuraldraft.io/v1/projects/me/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NEURALDRAFT_API_KEY"
```

```ts SDK
const usage = await nd.projects.usage();
```

</CodeGroup>

The admin shows the same under Settings → Credits, with what each credit was
used for.
