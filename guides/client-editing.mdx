---

You build the site; the client keeps it current. They can only change the
blocks that are editable (every text and picture on published pages, the
[marked blocks](/guides/editable-markup) on a deployed site), so the design
and code stay yours.

## Visual editor (Website → Edit site)

In the admin at [app.neuraldraft.io](https://app.neuraldraft.io):

1. **Website → Edit site** opens the live site with every editable text and
   picture highlighted.
2. Click a text to change it, click a picture to replace it. Changes are saved
   as drafts and stay private.
3. **Publish** puts the drafts live at once (no redeploy). **Discard** drops
   them.
4. Every publish can be **undone** from the history panel.
5. With several languages, a language picker edits each language's values.

**Website → Content** shows the same blocks as a list, page by page.
Pages, photos, forms and galleries have their own tabs under Website.

## By chat

The client adds the [hosted connector](/guides/hosted-connector) in Claude
(web, desktop, phone) or ChatGPT and signs in with their own email. Then:

> Change our opening hours to 6–14 and swap the hero photo for the one I just
> attached.

The assistant lists the editable blocks (`list_site_texts`), changes them
(`update_site_text`, after a confirmation) and links to the live page. A
connection only reaches the workspaces that person belongs to.

Pages published with `publish_site_page` can also be reverted
(`revert_site_page`), and a deployed site rolled back (`rollback_site`).

## Inviting the client

Settings → **Team** in the admin, or `invite_teammate` in the chat. Roles are
Admin and Member (a member can do everything except manage the team). For
agency clients the invitation goes out in the agency's name; see
[Agencies](/guides/agencies).
